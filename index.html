Checkmate Delivery

There's a town down the South Coast too small for most maps. A few streets pinched between the hills and the sea. Taxis hoot their way along the main road and kids kick their balls into the tide. When the fog comes in off the water, the whole place goes quiet.

Your sister Nandi never left. You did, for a while. Now you're back with a bike, a delivery box and rent to make. People need things moved. You move them.

Lately Nandi keeps going on about a bell.
